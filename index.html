    Projections you can actually trust

    A neural net projects weekly points from each player's recent form. It's scored the honest way — trained on 2016–22, tested on a season it never saw (2024) — and it beats what you'd get eyeballing the averages. Every projection ships with a floor and ceiling, because weekly fantasy is noisy and pretending otherwise is a lie.

    How do I get these?

    Reliable way (use a computer once):

    1. On a computer, open fantasy.espn.com in Chrome/Edge and sign in so you can see your league.
    2. Press F12 (or right-click → Inspect) → Application tab → left side Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com.
    3. Find espn_s2, copy its (long) Value. Find SWID, copy its Value (looks like {XXXX-…}).
    4. Paste both into the box above — order doesn't matter.

    On your phone only? Do the steps on a computer, then text/email the two values to yourself and paste them here. They're login tokens — send only to yourself.

    Shortcut (desktop bookmarklet): drag this to your bookmarks bar, open fantasy.espn.com logged in, click it — it copies both for you. If it says "not found", use the reliable way above.

    🍪 Grab ESPN cookies

    Open your league on sleeper.com — the long number in the URL is the League ID. Sleeper is fully public; no cookies needed.
